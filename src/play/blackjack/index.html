How to play

Get as close to 21 as you can without going over. Number cards are worth their number, face cards (Jacks, Queens and Kings) are worth 10, and Aces are worth 11 or 1, whichever helps you more.

Press Play to deal. Hit takes another card, Stand keeps what you have and lets the dealer play. Go over 21 and you bust.

21 with your first two cards is a BlackJack and wins right away. The dealer keeps drawing until they reach 17 or more. If you tie with the dealer, the dealer wins.

Press Play again to start a new hand. Good luck!
